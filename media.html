India Today

5 tips to overcome exam result blues

Contrary to what people would like you to believe, exam results are not the end of your life. Marks are one criterion — they do not reflect the talent you may have. Get up and get over the result blues.

18 Jul 2017 New Delhi

Lodestar's counsellors gave India Today readers five routes for students whose Class 10 or Class 12 results didn't go their way — a strengths and interest reset, a conversation about alternate boards or streams, a bridge activity while the next application cycle begins, a check on whether the result reflects effort or exam-day stress, and a plan for who in the family the student can turn to first.

A well-planned vacation includes a balanced mix of productive and leisure activities. Here's how to make the most of your free time after a hectic term.

13 Jul 2017 New Delhi

A term break is the best time to try one thing the school year doesn't allow. Lodestar recommended splitting the two weeks into three blocks — one for real rest, one for a real-world experience (shadowing a professional, a hands-on workshop, community work), and one for a self-directed learning goal the student picks themselves.
