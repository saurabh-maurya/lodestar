Refund Policy

What happens if a Lodestar program is not right for your family.

Before the first session

If you cancel before the first one-to-one session takes place, the program fee is refundable in full, less any transaction charges levied by the payment provider.

After sessions begin

Once counselling sessions have started, refunds are considered on a pro-rata basis against the sessions still outstanding. Written reports already delivered are not refundable.

Quality guarantee

Feedback is collected after every session. If you are unhappy, corrective action can include additional sessions, further information or a change of counsellor before a refund is considered.

Raising a request

Write to info@lodestar.guru or call +91 89715 20005. We acknowledge refund requests within two working days.

Last updated 2026. Questions about this policy? Write to info@lodestar.guru.
